Introduction

We are pleased to present BASF South America's 2024 Annual Report, highlighting key achievements across the environmental, social, and governance dimensions alongside our financial performance. The report also discusses the challenges we faced throughout the year, amid a complex macroeconomic environment that demanded a strategic and resilient approach to navigate the headwinds.

This report covers our initiatives in Brazil, Country Cluster Andes (CCA), and Country Cluster South (CCS). Where relevant, we also report on initiatives and guidelines from the BASF Group.

Message from Leadership

This report documents BASF in South America's key achievements and lessons learned during 2024. In a year that demanded focus, flexibility, and integration, we present our commitments, initiatives, and results as part of our journey toward more sustainable chemistry—one that is responsive to customer and regional market needs.

The global operating environment posed significant challenges, including rising raw material prices and heightened competition. In response, we strengthened operational discipline and intensified our drive for efficiency—without compromising innovation and sustainability as core drivers of our strategy. This combination has strengthened our presence in the region and helped make our business future-ready.

In South America, we sustained strong performance, supported by dedicated teams and our ability to adapt to local markets. We continued to invest in production integration, in diversifying our solutions, and in building partnerships that amplify our positive impact.

During the reporting period, we initiated a global reorganization of our portfolio into core businesses and standalone business units. This structure will boost efficiency, accelerate decision-making, and enhance the customer experience through management models tailored to the specific needs of each operation. The transformation is designed to increase BASF's competitiveness in a way that is sustainable and responsive to long-term challenges.

Sustainability is a core lever in our operations. In 2024, we expanded initiatives across circular economy, energy efficiency, and decarbonization, while building partnerships to accelerate low-carbon solutions. These initiatives are delivering on our commitment to the green transformation of the chemical industry—linking innovation with environmental responsibility to support our customers' green transition. South America, with its vast renewable energy potential and sustainable raw materials, plays a strategic role in this journey—and we are ready to lead the way.

Innovation remains central to our strategy. We invest in solutions that combine technology, science, and sustainability—turning challenges into opportunities. Artificial intelligence, for instance, is already driving operational efficiencies and expanding possibilities for our customers.

We reaffirm our commitment to diversity, equity, and inclusion. In 2024, we strengthened initiatives to expand minority representation and engage our partners in building a more diverse environment. We also made progress in wellbeing, with a special focus on mental health and the quality of life of current and future generations.

BASF proactively manages human rights risks, ensuring that our operations and supply chains align with the highest international standards. Our strengthened human rights governance reflects our commitment to fostering an ethical, transparent, and socially responsible business environment. We close this cycle with a sense of confidence in the path we have chosen and pride in all we achieved in 2024. Looking ahead to 2025, we remain guided by our strategic vision and focused on sustainable growth, innovation, and operational excellence—developing chemistry that drives positive transformation and helps build a more sustainable future. This journey is made possible by the dedication of our teams, collaboration with our suppliers, and the trust of the customers who choose us every day. Together, we will continue to strengthen BASF's role in South America as the preferred chemical company to enable our customers' green transformation.

Priscila Camara Senior Vice President, BASF South America

BASF's strategy is guided by an integrated view of the economic landscape, global trends, and the transformations shaping the chemical industry. Our approach is focused on customer needs, technological advances, and regulatory demands, with an overarching goal to create value sustainably.

Having set this strategic direction, BASF started a new cycle in September 2024 with the launch of our corporate strategy, "Winning Ways", built around four levers:

Focus: strengthen the core and unlock the value of standalone, while ensuring strategic capital allocation to maximize value creation.

Accelerate: simplify our organizational structure and increase corporate accountability.

Transform: drive sustainable-future solutions and net-zero measures at scale.

Win: step up speed, performance orientation, and a mindset of continuous improvement.

This model positions us to seize new growth opportunities and keeps BASF at the forefront of a more sustainable and competitive chemical industry—delivering greater value to customers through integrated, efficient, and needs-responsive solutions. This direction aligns with our global ambition to be the preferred chemical company to enable the green transformation of our customers.

A New Cycle. New Directions

As part of our new strategy, we have restructured our portfolio into two categories: core businesses and standalone businesses.

Core businesses include the Chemicals, Materials, Industrial Solutions, and Nutrition & Care segments—areas where BASF maintains strong integration and centralized management. Standalone businesses, such as Surface Technologies and Agricultural Solutions, now have greater flexibility and operational independence.

Through this new structure, our goal is to make each business more agile and responsive, supporting stronger alignment with the specific demands of each market.

Decorative Paints: A Strategic Choice

As part of our strategy to strengthen our chemicals portfolio and focus on businesses with greater global synergy, in 2024 we made the decision to sell our decorative paints business, including the Suvinil and Glasu! brands.

BASF closely follows macroeconomic factors that affect performance, and implements strategies to mitigate risks and capture opportunities. Global economic growth in 2024 was again driven more by services than the production of goods.

Declining inflation and the interest rate turnaround supported demand. Global chemical production grew by 3.9%, surpassing overall industrial production, despite regional variations in China and Europe. In this context, we strengthened competitiveness through a focus on cash generation, cost control, and investments—prioritizing markets like China and India.

In terms of financial performance, BASF posted global sales of €65.3 billion in 2024, a decline from €68.9 billion in the previous year, mainly due to lower prices as a result of stronger competition across all segments. Despite this challenging environment, EBITDA before special items increased by €187 million to €7.9 billion, driven by higher earnings in our core businesses.

Opportunities in South America

Fiscal 2024 was marked by stable raw material prices, logistical challenges, and stronger global competition, especially from Asian products. New regulations and the drive for greater energy efficiency also required structural adaptations.

In South America, Brazil experienced growth on the back of consumer demand and investments, despite adverse impacts in agribusiness; in Argentina, meanwhile, the economic environment remained challenging amid reforms.

Our performance reflected these regional contexts, with exchange rate effects impacting sales, partially offset by higher volumes in some businesses. Still, the region continues to show strong potential to lead the transition toward a low-carbon chemicals industry. We remain committed to investing in innovation, supply chains, and renewable raw materials, aligning our operations with local needs and global trends.

Results with Purpose

The year saw market adjustments that required efficient pricing and operational cost management. In South America, we focused efforts on optimizing cash flow, reducing inventory, and adjusting our production structure to match demand.

In agribusiness, volatility highlighted the need for strategic realignment to ensure competitiveness—this includes a new customer service model and investments in digitalization and sustainable agricultural solutions.

Innovation in Inoculation

BASF Argentina launched HiCoat® Dry, the first high-concentration, long-shelf-life solid inoculant for soybeans, developed at the Santo Tomé site, which specializes in biological seed treatment products. The solution delivers operational and environmental benefits by reducing transportation and storage volumes, simplifying application, and improving logistics efficiency through less waste and longer pre-inoculation windows.

Soil-based Technology

We developed a biobed system to make the disposal of pesticide residues more sustainable and accessible. This system enables farmers themselves to treat the rinse water from triple-washed pesticide containers directly on-site, using soil and naturally occurring microorganisms that break down chemical compounds. As a result, we eliminate the need to transport the waste to industrial treatment plants or incinerate it, creating a safer, lower-omission product lifecycle.

Soybeans and Bees in Productive Synergy

In a collaboration with the Brazilian Agricultural Research Corporation (EMBRAPA), we conducted a study showing that soybean farming and beekeeping can coexist in synergy, benefiting both producers and the environment. Adopting good agricultural and beekeeping practices supports pollinator preservation and enhances soybean yields, which saw an average increase of 13% in the study areas.

In addition to strengthening biodiversity, this interaction benefited beekeepers by doubling honey production compared to the national average.

AgroStart: driving Innovation in Agribusiness

AgroStart has continued to accelerate innovation in the agricultural sector by connecting startups and developing digital solutions that enhance efficiency and sustainability across the value chain. With more than 600 interactions with Latin American agtechs since 2016 and 22 active partnerships, the platform plays a key role in advancing digitalization and customer focus in agriculture.

Minha Fazenda BASF

In 2024, we launched Minha Fazenda BASF in Brazil, a new feature within the Fazenda BASF platform, created to provide farmers and agricultural professionals with personalized and easily accessible information. Featuring technical content, educational videos, market pricing, weather forecasts, and contact information for BASF distributors and consultants, this new tool connects digital capabilities with in-field agricultural needs.

Recognition for the initiative came quickly: Minha Fazenda BASF won two of Brazil's most prestigious communication awards. At the 50th ABERJE Awards, it was honored in the Digital Media category and at the 22nd ABMRA Agribusiness Communication Trade Show, it won gold in the Activations and Virtual Event Presence category. Integrated Environmental Approach

Risk Management and Safety Culture

We apply a rigorous approach to ensure safety, identifying risks and implementing preventive measures in all activities. Ongoing assessment of operational risks is integral to our culture, ensuring safe performance of field operations.

We maintain structured engagement initiatives, such as our Regional Safety Community, which brings together leaders to share best practices, and our Contractor Safety Forum, which promotes safety alignment with contractors. SIPATMA 2024: Safety and Sustainability in Focus

Improved Safety Indicators

Starting in 2025, we will adopt a new methodology for classifying incidents, considering only actual outcomes and excluding assessments based on potential severity. This change is designed to align with international standards and the guidelines of the International Council of Chemical Associations (ICCA), making reports more objective and standardized, while reinforcing transparency and continuous improvement in safety management.

Health Audits

In 2024, we conducted six medical audits across the region to strengthen ongoing occupational health monitoring.
